Part One — The Question

Two worlds, one seam.

He works at the seam between two worlds most people keep apart. On one side of it, he deploys LLM agents into production for real users. On the other, he competes in cloud-security challenges at a global level — and the agents he ships run on exactly the infrastructure he knows how to compromise. Which is why a single question follows him through everything he builds:

“How do we make autonomous AI systems trustworthy and secure, not just capable?

His work spans zero-trust architecture, SIEM and identity systems, and retrieval-grounded agents with human-in-the-loop safety. Ask him to compress all of it into one line, and he already has one ready.

“I build LLM agents you can trust — and break the cloud they run on.

— The thesis, in one line
